Laos Border Schedule (Bo Y) — 45D Visa-Free:

  1. 21:30: Bus departs 40 Hon Chong, Nha Trang (or 21:15 at 04 Tran Phu).
  2. 06:00: Arrive at Bo Y border. Breakfast/coffee on own account.
  3. 06:45: Driver collects passports to assist with exit stamping.
  4. 07:30: Passengers walk through facial scan, stamp into Laos and return.
  5. 08:00: Return to Vietnam immigration counter for re-entry stamp.
  6. 09:30: Bus departs back to Nha Trang.

📌 Border Cash Fees: Vietnam 50,000đ × 2 = 100,000đ | Laos 20,000đ × 2 = 40,000đ (Total: 140,000đ).

Laos Border Schedule (Bo Y) — 90D E-Visa:

  1. Bus departs Tue, Thu, Sun at 21:30.
  2. Exit Vietnam (50,000đ fee). Immediately photograph the exit stamp and send to EasyTrip.
  3. Cross Laos immigration and pay 20,000đ form/stamp fee.
  4. Wait at the neutral-zone café while EasyTrip processes the E-Visa (mobile PDF accepted at Bo Y).
  5. Once approved (~11:30 AM), re-enter Vietnam (50,000đ). Important: Tell the officer you have a 90D E-Visa!

Cambodia Schedule (Moc Bai) — 45D / 90D:

  1. Bus departs Tue, Thu, Sun at 21:30 from 40 Hon Chong.
  2. 06:30: Arrive at Moc Bai border; queue for 07:30 gate opening.
  3. Exit Vietnam (50k–150k VND). Send Exit Stamp photo before 08:00 AM.
  4. Cross to Cambodia gate (walk or motorbike taxi ~100k–150k VND).
  5. Purchase Cambodia visa at border (~$35–$50) unless all-inclusive package was booked.
  6. Wait ~4h at a café with Wi-Fi until EasyTrip sends the new Vietnam E-Visa (~11:30–12:00).
  7. Print the E-Visa on A4 paper, then queue for Vietnam re-entry (11:15–11:45).
