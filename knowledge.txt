1. VISARUN CONSULTATION LOGIC & ROUTING RULES
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Core objective: Collect exactly 5 pieces of information to match the bus route & departure date:
  1. Nationality: To recommend the correct border and check surcharges.
  2. Visa expiry date: To propose the departure date.
  3. Current city: To suggest the starting point (Nha Trang / Da Nang).
  4. Desired Visa type: Visa Free (45-day exemption) or 90-day Visa (E-visa).
  5. Contact phone number (Phone/Whatsapp/Zalo).

👉 DEPARTURE DATE RULE & SCHEDULE CONSTRAINTS:
   - The bus departure date MUST be 1 day BEFORE the customer's Visa expiry date.
   - The bus travels overnight, arriving at the border at 6:00 AM the next day (the expiry date) to complete procedures.
   - **Cambodia Route Constraint**: The bus to Cambodia departs ONLY on **Tuesdays, Thursdays, and Sundays**. Therefore, for Cambodia runs, the customer's Visa expiry date MUST fall on a **Monday, Wednesday, or Friday** (departure + 1 day). If it falls on another day, the customer must choose the nearest preceding Tuesday, Thursday, or Sunday departure date.

👉 NATIONALITY & BORDER CHECKPOINT CLASSIFICATION:
   - Citizens of Russia (Nga), South Korea (Hàn Quốc), Belarus, Malaysia, Czech Republic, and other ASEAN countries => Recommend LAOS route (Bo Y border).
     * Reason: These nationalities enjoy visa exemption for Laos, saving the cost of a Laos visa.
   - Citizens of USA (Mỹ), Canada, Australia, UK (Anh), Ukraine, Kyrgyz, Brazil, Uzbekistan, and others => Recommend CAMBODIA route (Moc Bai border).

👉 VIETNAM VISA-EXEMPTION & PACKAGE AVAILABILITY RULE:
   - **Only** customers whose nationalities are visa-exempt for Vietnam (e.g., Russia, South Korea, Japan, Belarus, UK, France, Germany, Italy, Spain, ASEAN, etc.) can select the "45D Visa Free" (45 ngày miễn thị thực) package.
   - Customers whose nationalities are **NOT** visa-exempt for Vietnam (e.g., USA, Canada, Australia, China, India, etc.) **CANNOT** select the "45D Visa Free" package. They are restricted to "90-day E-visa Single Entry" or "90-day E-visa Multi Entry".

6. END-TO-END 7-STEP OPERATIONAL SOP WORKFLOW
-------------------------------------------------------------------
This section outlines the exact steps our operators/staff must follow when coordinating a visa run:

Step 1: Date & Route Matching
- Parse the customer's nationality. Check Vietnam visa-exemption status.
- If not exempt (e.g. USA, Canada, Australia):
  * Chặn gói "Free Visa 45 ngày". Chỉ áp dụng gói E-visa 90 ngày (Single/Multi).
  * Đi tuyến Campuchia (không được đi Lào do không miễn visa Lào).
  * Lịch xe đi Campuchia chỉ khởi hành tối Thứ 3, Thứ 5, Chủ Nhật.
  * Hạn visa cũ bắt buộc phải rơi vào Thứ 2, Thứ 4, hoặc Thứ 6.

Step 2: Seat Map & Locking (Sơ đồ ghế & Khóa chỗ)
- Show the interactive seat map (A1-A20, B1-B20) to the customer to let them choose their seat.
- Once a seat is selected, execute the Telegram "Lock ghế" command:
  Format: [Ngày khởi hành] - [Loại dịch vụ] [Tên khách] [Số ghế] [Điểm đón] [Nguồn] [Trạng thái: Unpaid].
- Keep the seat locked while waiting for payment.

Step 3: Payment & Document Collection (Thanh toán & Thu thập thông tin)
- Send the bank payment details (Vietcombank - EASY TRIP & VISA CO. LTD - 1068582577) along with the QR code.
- Pricing rules:
  * For Free Visa: Customer only pays the bus ticket (1,400,000 VND).
  * For E-visa packages: Customer pays both the bus ticket and the E-visa fee.
  * If E-visa is required: Request the customer to send a clear passport photo and a portrait photo.
  * Children & No-bus rules:
    - Children under 9 years old (trẻ em < 9 tuổi): Free bus ticket (nằm chung cabin với bố mẹ). They only pay the visa portion.
    - Visa-only (no bus) price for Russia, Ukraine, Kyrgyzstan, Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, Tajikistan, Belarus, and other CIS: 2,000,000 VND (90D visa).
    - Visa-only (no bus) price for others: Package price - 1,400,000 VND.

Step 4: Receipt Stamping & Confirmation (Đóng dấu Receipt)
- Once payment is confirmed, send a final confirmation message.
- The operator must print/screenshot the payment receipt, stamp it with the official "Easy Trip & Visa Co. Ltd" company stamp (đóng dấu mộc đỏ công ty), photograph it, and send it to the customer.

Step 5: E-visa Submission & Code Sharing (Nộp hồ sơ & Gửi mã Zalo)
- The operator manually registers the customer's E-visa on the public service portal of the Ministry of Public Safety (Cổng dịch vụ công Bộ Công An) and pays the fee.
- Once submitted, copy the E-visa application code.
- Send this code to the Zalo group for Mr. Luân so he can push and approve the visa on time.
- Timeline logic:
  * Customer boards the bus on the evening of Day X (e.g., departure at 21:30).
  * Customer arrives at the border at 6:00 - 7:00 AM on Day X+1.
  * Expected re-entry into Vietnam is around 8:00 AM on Day X+1.
  * The visa must be processed as an Urgent 4-hour E-visa.
  * Approved visa must be returned to the customer between 8:00 AM and 11:30 AM on Day X+1.

Step 6: Visa Return & Re-entry Instructions (Trả E-visa & Hướng dẫn cửa khẩu)
- Once approved, send the E-visa PDF/link to the customer via private message.
- Border-specific rules:
  * At Moc Bai (Cambodia): Customer MUST print the E-visa on paper to present to border control.
  * At Bo Y (Laos): No print required (digital copy on phone is accepted).
- Departure warning:
  * Remind the customer that the return bus departs at 13:00 (1:00 PM) sharp.
  * If the immigration queue to enter Vietnam is too long, reassure the customer: "Yên tâm đi, chúng tôi đã dặn tài xế, xe buýt sẽ vẫn đợi các bạn." (Rest assured, we have instructed the driver; the bus will wait for you).

Step 7: Evaluation & Review Request (Đánh giá Google Maps)
- Once the customer is safely back on the bus, check the completeness of all operations.
- If everything went smoothly and customer satisfaction is high, send the Google Maps review link (https://g.page/r/CREMymPQwmBuEBI/review) to request a positive review.
